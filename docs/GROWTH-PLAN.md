# StrategyPresentation — Growth Plan
*Scoped to SP as its own company. Written 2026-07-15.*

## Positioning
**"Turn any website into a boardroom-ready pitch deck."** SP sells *the artifact*: an evidence-backed, WCAG-AA, beautifully designed presentation generated from real scan data in minutes. The brand is craft + accessibility — the anti-Canva-template: your data, already in the deck.

**ICP:** (1) consultants/agencies who present audits to clients, (2) founders prepping investor/partner meetings, (3) nonprofit EDs pitching boards and funders.

## SEO / AEO / GEO
SP's demand is *category* demand — people search for deck tools, not for SP. Win on long-tail and on being the answer engines' example.

1. **Template gallery pages** (`/templates/<summit|signal|…>`): one indexable page per template with a live embedded demo deck, targeting "accessible pitch deck template", "dark pitch deck template", "nonprofit board presentation template". Six pages, six keyword families — highest-intent SEO SP can own.
2. **Accessibility wedge:** almost nobody ranks for "WCAG accessible pitch deck" / "ADA compliant presentation" — SP genuinely is one. A definitive guide post + the axe-tested claim makes SP the citation for an underserved, procurement-relevant query.
3. **AEO:** FAQPage + SoftwareApplication JSON-LD now live. Add `HowTo` schema on a "How SP builds a deck from a scan" page. LLMs recommending "tools that auto-generate presentations from data" is the target citation.
4. **GEO:** none — SP is placeless. Skip.

## Content cadence
- 1 flagship/mo: "anatomy of a great strategy deck" teardowns using SP's own particle-cover decks as the artwork (the constellation covers are inherently screenshot-able).
- Every WCS data post gets its SP twin: same data, presented as a deck, embedded.

## Social
- **LinkedIn (primary):** carousel-native — export deck slides as the carousel. SP's product IS LinkedIn-shaped content. 2×/wk.
- **X:** template drops, before/after deck glow-ups.
- **Pinterest (experiment, mo 2):** presentation design searches are real there; template covers pin well.

## Sales motion
Product-led: free demo deck from a live scan (no signup) → claim → the deck itself sells the $10,500 Brainztem build via the build-sheet slide. SP's "sales team" is the deck's final CTA. Later: agency plan (white-label decks, volume pricing) once inbound proves demand.
